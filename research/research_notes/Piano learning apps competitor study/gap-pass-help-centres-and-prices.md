# Gap pass: help centres, kids positioning and prices for piano-learning apps (browser read, 2026-11-01)

Method: real Chrome via chrome-cdp, about 40 page reads, no logins. Pages read on 2026-11-01. Where a page shows its own date it is quoted. Anything older than 2024 is marked (PRE-2024). Official statements are separated from reviewer opinion.

## 1. Wrong-note and chord handling on the lesson screen

### Takeaway
Only Simply Piano documents wrong-note feedback officially (colour coding, red in moving stages only, correct key hinted in yellow). Flowkey and Skoove help centres had no page on it; Flowkey's store listing only says Wait Mode "waits for you to hit the right notes and chords". Yousician says only that "Play for me" can sound the note or chord, and that Play mode rewards timing.

### Cited Findings
- Simply Piano (official): the app listens and gives live feedback; if it hears mistakes it colour-codes notes and shows a visual keyboard at the bottom. Blue = correct with no hint, Yellow = correct after a hint, Red (moving stages only) = wrong note. Grey keys show notes the app hears; if you played wrongly the correct key is highlighted yellow. Page dated 14 Sep 2026. — [Simply help: The basics](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/7943490-learning-with-simply-piano-the-basics)
- Flowkey (official store text): Wait Mode "follows as you play and waits for you to hit the right notes and chords", works by microphone or Bluetooth/MIDI. "Just Play" keeps up with the score "even if you miss a few notes". Store page updated 16 Oct 2026. — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flowkey.app&hl=en&gl=GB)
- Yousician piano (official): in Practice mode "Play for me" lets you hear what the note or chord should sound like. In Play mode the multiplier starts at 3x and rises up to 5x by hitting notes correctly and with good timing. — [Practice and Play modes in piano](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204796991-Practice-and-Play-modes-in-piano)
- Reviewer opinion on Simply Piano, Google Play UK: one reviewer (26 Aug 2026) says that if you make the slightest mistake you must replay the practice bit again and again; another (28 Apr 2026) complains the app gives about 4 seconds to play a note and sometimes never hears a note. Individual reviews, unverified. — [Google Play UK listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.joytunes.simplypiano&hl=en_GB&gl=GB)
- Common Sense Media on Piano Maestro (2015, PRE-2024): kids "have to play the right notes as they scroll by"; the app gives instant visual and auditory feedback; reviewer wished feedback addressed tricky passages and fingerings. — [CSM Piano Maestro](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/piano-maestro-by-joytunes)

### Inferences
- Simply's red state exists only in "moving stages", which suggests slow lesson steps hint rather than punish. The reviewer comments suggest the moving stages repeat on error, but that is not stated officially.

### Gaps
- Chord judging (all keys vs any key) is not documented on any page read, for any app.
- Whether wait mode has a fail state: no official statement found for flowkey, Skoove or Yousician.
- Skoove's and Flowkey's on-screen error visuals and sounds: not found in their help centres.

## 2. Finger numbers

### Takeaway
Yousician documents finger numbers/colours and offers four notation views. Simply replaces finger numbers with hand icons for kids. Flowkey's store text says video shows "how to place your fingers". Nothing found on Skoove.

### Cited Findings
- Yousician: four notation options (enhanced, falling notes, colored, sheet), changed from Pause menu > Options. Enhanced and Colored use finger colours (thumb green, index yellow, middle purple, ring blue, little red). Falling notes show finger numbers 1-5 and blue left / purple right hand; note names included. Standard sheet is black and white. Support page footer shows 2024, so the article may be 2024-era. — [Notation options in piano](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/208678605-Notation-options-in-piano)
- Simply Piano: kids aged 4-10 get "hand icons instead of standard finger numbering in the lessons" for the first few courses (up to Essentials II); dated 15 Jun 2026. — [Is the app experience different for children?](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/8053114-is-the-app-experience-different-for-children)
- Flowkey store text: Video shows "how to place your fingers". — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flowkey.app&hl=en&gl=GB)

### Gaps
- Whether finger numbers can be switched off in Simply, flowkey, Skoove: not found. In Yousician, switching notation is the way to change them (inference from the four views).

## 3. Loop and section practice

### Takeaway
Yousician is the only app with a documented loop mechanism: drag sliders on the bottom progress bar, with an optional one-bar count-in. Flowkey lists "Loop: choose specific sections ... replay until perfected".

### Cited Findings
- Yousician: "You can loop any section by moving the sliders" on the progress bar at the bottom. Practice mode "restarts at the end so you can keep practicing". "Loop lead-in" gives a one-bar count-in at restart. Left/Right hand toggle for two-hand songs. — [Practice and Play modes in piano](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204796991-Practice-and-Play-modes-in-piano)
- Flowkey: Loop feature, Select a Hand (right/left separately). — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flowkey.app&hl=en&gl=GB)
- Simply Piano PLAY feature list: Listen, Teach Me, Play, Feedback, Auto Scroll, My Library; no loop listed. Reviewer (26 Aug 2026, opinion) says practice bits cannot be slowed, only the whole song after completing all bits. — [Understanding PLAY](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/7943680-understanding-play); [Google Play UK](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.joytunes.simplypiano&hl=en_GB&gl=GB)

### Gaps
- Flowkey handle/bar-number mechanics, Skoove loop: not documented in pages read.

## 4. Tempo options

### Takeaway
Yousician's 25-125 % in 5 % steps is confirmed on an official page. Simply offers tempo in moving stages; a kids reward goes up to 200 %. Skoove: pause, tap Tempo.

### Cited Findings
- Yousician: "Auto-adjust speed ... works from 25% to 125% in 5% increments. You can also change the tempo by moving the slider." — [Practice and Play modes in piano](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204796991-Practice-and-Play-modes-in-piano)
- Skoove: "Simply pause the lesson, tap 'Tempo' & choose your tempo." Not available in "Get to know the song" and a few other steps. Page dated 23 Jun 2023 (PRE-2024). — [Can I adjust the tempo?](https://help.skoove.com/en/articles/5421914-can-i-adjust-the-tempo-of-the-pieces-on-skoove)
- Simply Piano: tempo options appear in moving stages of "Teach Me" and "Play" (top left); "Play" and "Feedback" are iOS only. Kids reward "Tempo Rush" plays a song at faster speeds up to 200 % (iOS only). Google Play "What's new" says "slow down the music till you get it right". — [Understanding PLAY](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/7943680-understanding-play); [XP and rewards](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/15913867-how-do-xp-and-rewards-work-kids-profiles)
- Skoove vendor advice (instructors' opinion): practise slowly. — [Practice tips](https://help.skoove.com/en/articles/5421912-do-you-have-any-practice-tips-to-maximize-the-progress-i-can-make)

### Gaps
- Exact tempo values for flowkey, Skoove, Simply; Hoffman not checked.

## 5. Per-song difficulty levels

### Cited Findings
- Flowkey: "songs available in four difficulty levels". — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flowkey.app&hl=en&gl=GB)
- Simply Piano PLAY: songs come in Essentials, Intermediate and Pre-Advanced levels (three). — [Understanding PLAY](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/7943680-understanding-play)
- Yousician piano: no per-song count found; the learning path has 9 complete levels in the Classical and Pop themes and 5 in the Knowledge theme. (The "Song screen" article that mentions Simplified/Original/Tabs is GuitarTuna/guitar, not piano.) — [Piano learning path](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204697992-How-the-piano-learning-path-works); [Song screen](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/4419297148817-Song-screen)

### Gaps
- How each flowkey level simplifies the arrangement (melody/chords/both hands): not on pages read.

## 6. Scoring and progression

### Cited Findings
- Simply stars: up to 3 stars per song in star levels; a counter like 0/6 shows stars earned versus needed to continue (31 Aug 2026). — [Understanding Star Levels](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/7943591-understanding-star-levels)
- Simply PLAY unlocks after 80 % of Essentials III or 30 % of any later course; subscription required. — [Understanding PLAY](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/7943680-understanding-play)
- Simply 5-minute workouts: a new one each week; unlock after the first two Piano Basics lessons (first 3 notes); three options: Flash card challenge, Song library training, On the go stages. Some players "may no longer see" them because newer practice features replace them (6 Aug 2026). — [5-minute workouts](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/7939170-what-are-5-minute-workouts)
- Simply streaks: complete at least one practice session each week; start with 2 streak freezes, earn one more per 5 consecutive weeks; freezes apply automatically (15 Jun 2026). — [Streaks](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/15496066-how-do-streaks-work-in-simply-piano)
- Simply kids XP/levels (ages 4-10, iOS only): XP for lessons and library songs, XP value shown before a song, higher scores level up faster, rewards unlocked (Tempo Rush, Song Mix) (12 Jul 2026). — [XP and rewards](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/15913867-how-do-xp-and-rewards-work-kids-profiles)
- Yousician: points multiplier 3x to 5x in Play mode; stars and points as rewards; each task in a mission must be played to complete it; the path is unlocked so you can skip to any mission. Weekly Activity report emailed every Monday to the account email (notes played, total play time). — [Practice and Play modes](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204796991-Practice-and-Play-modes-in-piano); [Learning path](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204697992-How-the-piano-learning-path-works)
- Flowkey: no scoring rules found; "Just Play" tolerates misses. — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flowkey.app&hl=en&gl=GB)
- Skoove vendor advice: simple goals (2-3 bars a day), about 15 min every two days at the start. Not a scoring rule. — [Practice tips](https://help.skoove.com/en/articles/5421912-do-you-have-any-practice-tips-to-maximize-the-progress-i-can-make)

### Gaps
- Star thresholds / accuracy %, lives, leaderboards: not found for any app on pages read.

## 7. Parent controls and kids positioning

### Cited Findings
- Skoove (official, 30 Jul 2025): "designed for adult beginners, kids aged 7 and up, and parents who want to learn together." — [Who can use Skoove?](https://help.skoove.com/en/articles/5417621-who-can-use-skoove-can-children-learn-with-skoove)
- Simply Piano (official marketing): ad-free, "no interactions with strangers", parents can hide songs; up to 5 profiles; Trinity College London partner; "+5000 songs". — [Piano for kids](https://www.hellosimply.com/family/piano-for-kids)
- Simply: kids aged 4-10 get a customised experience in the first courses (to Essentials II). Kids profiles (4-10) have XP (iOS only). — [Children's experience](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/8053114-is-the-app-experience-different-for-children)
- Simply plans: Individual (1 premium profile), Duo (2, Simply Piano subscribers only), Family (all 5 profiles premium, all apps); dated 15 Jun 2026. — [Choose a plan](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/8343647-choose-an-individual-duo-or-family-plan)
- Flowkey Family: separate Premium accounts for up to 5 people (Play listing); help centre: Family includes up to five user profiles and everything in Premium. — [Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flowkey.app&hl=en&gl=GB); [Plans](https://help.flowkey.com/en/articles/4466337-which-subscription-plans-are-available)
- Yousician Family: 4 Premium+ accounts, personal private accounts under one plan, first 7 days free. — [Family plan](https://yousician.com/family)
- Common Sense Media, Piano Maestro (2015, PRE-2024): age 9+, iPad; "Activities, animations get older kids and tween practicing"; can get expensive without a free teacher account; asks if over 13 with no verification; teachers can see student progress and assign Home Challenges; verdict: "a fun, approachable way for kids to practice the piano, but it isn't cheap", visuals suit younger kids; scrolling notes, mascot, metronome, music can partly be turned off. — [CSM](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/piano-maestro-by-joytunes)
- Common Sense Media also lists "Hello Piano - Lessons & Games" age 8+ (2019, PRE-2024). A search for "piano app" showed no review of flowkey, Simply Piano, Skoove, Yousician or Hoffman Academy on result page 1 of 2. — [CSM search](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/search/piano%20app?f%5B0%5D=type%3Aapp)

### Gaps
- Store minimum ages (Google Play content rating): not captured; the Play page text had no age line.
- Screen-time controls and parent progress emails: none documented for flowkey, Skoove, Hoffman. Only Yousician's weekly Activity report to the account email.
- CSM search page 2 not read; the guessed direct URL for Simply Piano returned 404.

## 8. Official prices (GBP where found)

### Cited Findings
- Skoove (official help centre, 11 Feb 2026), with GBP: 1 month GBP 24.99/month; 3 months GBP 16.66/month (GBP 49.99 every 3 months); 12 months GBP 10.41/month (GBP 124.99 every 12 months, single upfront charge). Lifetime plan on request via support; 14-day extended trial via a link. — [What is Skoove Premium?](https://help.skoove.com/en/articles/5417607-what-is-skoove-premium-and-how-much-does-it-cost)
- Skoove pricing page as shown to this browser, in EUR: 29.99 monthly, 19.99/month billed quarterly (59.99), 12.49/month billed yearly (149.99); 7-day free trial on the 12-month plan; lifetime on request. Page showed EUR, not GBP. — [Skoove pricing](https://www.skoove.com/en/pricing)
- Flowkey (help centre, USD example only): Classic $16.49/mo or $99.99/yr; Premium $24.99/mo or $149.99/yr; Family $37.49/mo or $224.99/yr. Yearly plans include a 7-day trial; 14-day money-back; no lifetime plan; "prices vary by country"; only Premium is sold via App Store/Play, Classic and Family via web. No GBP figure found. flowkey.com/en/pricing returned "Page not found". — [Which plans are available?](https://help.flowkey.com/en/articles/4466337-which-subscription-plans-are-available)
- Hoffman Academy (USD only): Basic free forever; Premium monthly $24/month, yearly $20/month billed at $239; extra family members $12/mo or $119/yr; 30-day money back; prices exclude tax. /pricing redirects to /premium. No GBP. — [Hoffman Premium](https://www.hoffmanacademy.com/premium)
- Simply Piano (official): no fixed price published; price depends on country, platform and plan; check in app (Menu > Join Simply). The 7-day free trial is only on the yearly plan, one per account/Apple ID. Free: Piano Basics course and Touch Intro. hellosimply.com/pricing is a 404. — [Subscribe to Simply Piano](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/2728030-subscribe-to-simply-piano); [Free content](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/11056528-what-can-i-access-for-free-in-simply-piano)
- Simply Piano reviewer figure (opinion, unverified): a UK reviewer on Google Play says they paid GBP 179 for a subscription (26 Aug 2026). Not a list price. — [Google Play UK](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.joytunes.simplypiano&hl=en_GB&gl=GB)
- Yousician: plans are Premium (Yousician songs), Premium+ Personal (adds popular songs), Premium+ Family (up to 4); monthly or yearly; the page has no figures. yousician.com/pricing is 404. — [Premium plans](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005189525-Yousician-Premium-plans)

### Gaps
- Google Play UK in-app-purchase price lists are not shown in the page text for flowkey or Simply Piano. No GBP figures captured for flowkey, Simply, Yousician, Hoffman. Yousician Play listing not read.

## 9. Piano Maestro and Hoffman Academy status

### Cited Findings
- Piano Maestro: Simply's 2026 Google Play text still describes Simply (formerly JoyTunes) as "creators of award-winning apps Piano Maestro and Piano Dust Buster". hellosimply.com/piano-maestro is a 404, and Piano Maestro is not in the Simply apps menu (Piano, Draw, Guitar, Sing, Tune). Current maintenance status not confirmed. — [Google Play UK](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.joytunes.simplypiano&hl=en_GB&gl=GB); [Simply site](https://www.hellosimply.com/simply-piano)
- Piano Maestro kid-facing screen (CSM, 2015, PRE-2024): Journey of 38 chapters, Library (13 beginner method books), Home Challenge; scrolling Guitar Hero-style notes, dancing mascot, badges. — [CSM](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/piano-maestro-by-joytunes)
- Hoffman Academy: 400+ video lessons, downloadable sheet music, interactive learning games, practice tools, daily guided practice sessions with audio play-along tracks; games feature characters (Rae, Iggy). Free tier gives video lessons only; games and practice tools are Premium. — [Hoffman Premium](https://www.hoffmanacademy.com/premium)

### Gaps
- Hoffman app platforms, any wrong-note detection (it looks video-and-games based; inference only), and Piano Maestro store status in 2026 not verified.
