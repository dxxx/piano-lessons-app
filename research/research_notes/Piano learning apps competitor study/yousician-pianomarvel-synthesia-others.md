# Second-tier piano-learning apps (Yousician, Piano Marvel, Playground Sessions, Pianote, Melodics, Synthesia, Android alternatives) - as of 2026-11-01

Method note: about 18 search/fetch calls. Several official help pages (Yousician support, Google Play listings) returned 403 or truncated content, so many mechanics are listed under Gaps. Prices are shown as found with currency and source; aggregator prices are flagged.

## 1. Lesson/song screen: falling vs sheet, cues, fingering, colours, right/wrong handling

### Takeaway
Synthesia is the clearest reference for a falling-note plus optional-notation screen with per-hand colours, finger numbers on blocks and dot cues on keys. Playground Sessions and Piano Marvel are sheet-music-first (notes turn green/red as played); Melodics uses timing colours. Yousician-piano screen details could not be verified from official pages.

### Cited Findings
- Synthesia: notes are falling blocks; left hand usually blue, right hand usually green; play each note as its block reaches the top of the on-screen keyboard. - [Synthesia first-song guide](https://synthesia.app/support/guide/firstSong)
- Synthesia finger hints: a number on a block (thumb 1, pinky 5); unnumbered notes inferred from stable hand position; a hint usually signals a hand move or pattern change. - [Synthesia first-song guide](https://synthesia.app/support/guide/firstSong)
- Synthesia Melody Practice: coloured dots appear on the keys you should play; the app holds until the correct note is pressed. - [Synthesia first-song guide](https://synthesia.app/support/guide/firstSong); [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/synthesia/id579518445)
- Synthesia display options: sheet music, falling notes, or both; notation optional per song; "Song Only" keyboard (just the keys the song uses) vs "My Keyboard"; pinch to zoom. - [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/synthesia/id579518445); [Synthesia home](https://www.synthesiagame.com/)
- Synthesia "Parts": each track is a Part; per-Part hand = Left / Right (you practise it) / Background (Synthesia plays it) / Custom. Per-Part colour per interaction mode; Background defaults to Hidden; Hidden combined with Left/Right makes practice harder; sounded/muted toggle; "Auto Color" = left blue, right green, background hidden; drag a setting onto "All Parts" to apply to everything. - [Synthesia song setup guide](https://synthesia.app/support/guide/songSetup)
- Synthesia Free Play shows coloured squares rising from pressed keys. - [Yamaha Hub, secondary snippet](https://hub.yamaha.com/?p=44644)
- Playground Sessions: interactive lessons with sheet music that follows your playing; correct notes turn green, misses red; video lessons with Harry Connick Jr.; can switch between simpler and more complex arrangements. - [Deviant Noise review](https://deviantnoise.net/education/piano/playground-sessions-review/); colour claim via [inkl/MusicRadar](https://www.inkl.com/news/playground-sessions-review)
- Melodics: notes change colour for timing - green correct timing, red missed. - [Melodics press clipping](https://melodics.rockpaperscissors.biz/artist/19108/press_clips/28857/white)
- Perfect Piano: falling-note visuals, auto / semi-auto / note-pause modes, USB MIDI, multiplayer weekly song challenge (App Store description, may differ on Android). - [mwm.ai listing summary](https://mwm.ai/apps/perfect-piano-learn-to-play/942937409)
- Simpia: instant per-note correct/incorrect feedback plus rhythm feedback; MIDI and Bluetooth MIDI supported. - [Simpia App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6444191617)
- Piano Marvel: sheet-music lessons with real-time pitch detection and Sight Reading Factory integration (2026 review). - [Homeschool Picks, via search snippet](https://homeschoolpicks.com/electives/piano-marvel-review-homeschool/?format=md)

### Inferences
- For a 6-year-old on an on-screen keyboard: Synthesia's pattern (falling block + dot on the target key + optional finger number + per-hand colour + Background hand played by the app) maps directly. With exact key knowledge, green/red feedback on the key itself is trivial.
- Synthesia's "Background" Part concept is a cheap way to do hands-separate from MIDI tracks.

### Gaps
- Yousician piano lesson-screen layout, finger numbers, chord handling: official help article blocked (403).
- How any product treats wrong notes inside chords: not found.
- Piano Academy and tonestro: no feature info found at all; Perfect Piano only secondary.

## 2. Practice tools: wait, tempo, loop, hands, metronome, performance mode, backing

### Takeaway
Synthesia has the fullest documented set (Melody Practice wait, hands, speed +/-, loops, bookmarks, metronome, timeline jump). Yousician piano has Practice vs Play modes with auto-adjusting tempo (25-125%). Melodics has Memory Mode tied to Platinum.

### Cited Findings
- Synthesia tools: Melody Practice (waits), hands separate/together with the other hand played by the app, loops, bookmarks, built-in metronome, fingering hints, lighted-keyboard cues. - [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/synthesia/id579518445)
- Synthesia speed: + and - buttons beside the BPM indicator; guide advises starting slow enough to be accurate. Tapping the timeline jumps to a point (useful for one section). The first-song guide does not describe loops. - [Synthesia first-song guide](https://synthesia.app/support/guide/firstSong)
- Synthesia 10.7 (2021) added bookmarks and loops shown in sheet music; 10.9 (Dec 2022) added pedal in recordings and Roman-numeral chord names (pre-2024). - [App Store version history](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/synthesia/id579518445)
- Synthesia Melody Practice scoring described as tempo-independent (correct notes and duration accuracy). - [Yamaha Hub, secondary](https://hub.yamaha.com/?p=44644)
- Yousician piano: two modes, Practice and Play; Play gives stars/points; auto-adjust changes tempo between 25% and 125% in 5% steps. - [Yousician piano help, text seen only via search summary](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204796991-Practice-and-Play-modes-in-piano)
- Yousician "Wait To Play" toggle holds playback until the correct note (documented for guitar; piano not confirmed). - [Yousician guitar help](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/201558362-Practice-vs-Play-mode-Guitar-)
- Melodics: Memory Mode tied to Platinum (older support table). - [Melodics support](https://www.melodics.com/support-articles/support-scoring)
- Playground Sessions: slow-down available; reviewer found no loop or wait mode documented; third party describes solo practice, band accompaniment and Performance modes. - [Deviant Noise](https://deviantnoise.net/education/piano/playground-sessions-review/); [All Access Group](https://allaccessgroup.com/blog/playground-sessions-the-untraditional-piano-teacher/)
- Piano Marvel SASR plays straight through at a steady tempo without stopping, so notes at the wrong time lower the score (no-stop test). - [Piano Marvel SASR](https://pianomarvel.com/feature/sasr)

### Inferences
- A kid app can pair "wait" practice (no pressure) with a play-through that steps tempo in 5% increments like Yousician.

### Gaps
- How a loop section is chosen (UX) in any product: not found.
- Piano Marvel practice modes (wait, loop, hands): not found.
- "Practice makes perfect" repetition logic: not found.

## 3. Scoring and progression

### Takeaway
Yousician: one silver to three gold stars from accuracy+timing. Melodics: timing precision + note streaks, stars 1-3 plus Platinum, no ceiling. Piano Marvel SASR: adaptive 100-1900 score. Playground: accuracy/rhythm badges, criticised as unforgiving.

### Cited Findings
- Yousician: accuracy and timing set score and stars, one silver to three gold per song part; gold needs every note with good timing. - [Yousician scoring article](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/208014795-How-song-score-is-calculated) (seen via search summary)
- Yousician "lives" claim is only from a third-party review, not confirmed officially. - [Violet Recording](https://violetrecording.com/yousician-review/) (opinion)
- Melodics: score = timing precision + consecutive-correct streaks; a miss resets the streak; no ceiling (can exceed 100); stars 1/2/3/Platinum (Platinum = all green); each lesson has its own range. Thresholds not given. - [Melodics lesson scoring](https://support.melodics.com/en/articles/15092017-lesson-scoring)
- Older Melodics table: 50% one star, 75% two, 90% three, 100% Platinum; may be outdated and conflicts with "no ceiling". - [Melodics support](https://www.melodics.com/support-articles/support-scoring)
- Piano Marvel SASR: score 100-1900, each 100 = a level; 90 sublevels in 18 levels (page also says "90 levels"); excerpts chosen adaptively by predictive models; difficulty calibrated summer 2020 by a sight-reading team; free with an account; 1,582,668 tests taken, average 404, 2026 world top score 1,863; 30-day challenge about 12 min/day. - [Piano Marvel SASR](https://pianomarvel.com/feature/sasr)
- Non-piano-major college average SASR 429 (Oct 2022, pre-2024). - [Piano Marvel article](https://pianomarvel.com/article/the-ultimate-sight-reading-test)
- Playground Sessions: accuracy readout after each section; badges at accuracy/rhythm levels; Trustpilot reviewer says grading demands near-perfect notes and timing. - [inkl/MusicRadar](https://www.inkl.com/news/playground-sessions-review); [Trustpilot](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/playgroundsessions.com)
- Synthesia: per-performance feedback and long-term progress tracking; no stars or leaderboards documented. - [Synthesia home](https://www.synthesiagame.com/)

### Inferences
- Streak scoring and 3 stars are easy to adapt; Melodics-style reset-on-miss may be too harsh for age 6.

### Gaps
- The Piano Marvel "99% polyphony" claim was NOT found on any page read (the SASR page says nothing on polyphony). Unverified.
- Yousician star % thresholds, leaderboards, daily goals, skill trees, difficulty gating: not retrieved.

## Borrowable ideas summary (inference)
- Synthesia: per-hand colours, Background hand auto-played, dot on target key, optional finger numbers, tap timeline to jump section, +/- tempo.
- Yousician: auto tempo in 5% steps (25-125%), 3-star outcome.
- Melodics: streak scoring; Platinum (all green) unlocks Memory Mode (hidden cues).
- Piano Marvel: adaptive excerpt selection with calibrated levels (needs data; not feasible at launch).
